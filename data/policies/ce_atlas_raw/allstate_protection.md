## Coverage
Standard Plans cover product breakdowns and malfunctions during normal use. Accident Plans also cover product breakdowns and malfunctions during normal use as well as life's accidents like drops and spills, including liquid damage from immersion.

## Eligibility and Term
An Allstate Protection Plan must be bought within 30 days of item purchase. Accidental Damage Protection Plans are only eligible for new items. The plan term (when it starts and how long it lasts) is shown on your plan confirmation.

## Fees / Deductible
Plans cover 100% of parts and labor; a deductible may apply. For phones, a flat $149 deductible applies per approved claim.

## Resolution
Items are repaired, or the full item price is paid back to you if Allstate Protection Plans can't fix it; a cash settlement for replacement cost may be provided. Many claims are approved instantly; in other cases, a specialist will guide you through next steps.

## Exclusions
Neither plan covers intentional damage, loss, theft, or commercial use. Cosmetic damage and normal wear and tear are not covered.

## Making a Claim
File online; proof of purchase and the plan confirmation are required.
