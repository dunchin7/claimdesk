# AppleCare+ for iPhone — Key Terms

> (Short excerpts for analysis; not the full contract.)

## Section 2 — Plan Term
For Single-Pay Plans, your Plan Term is two (2) years (or three (3) years for Apple Watch Edition).

## Section 3.1 — Hardware Coverage (defects)
If during the Plan Term you submit a valid claim for a hardware defect in materials or workmanship, Apple will repair the defect at no charge, using new parts or parts that are equivalent to new. Coverage includes the battery when the capacity of the Covered Device's battery to hold an electrical charge is less than eighty percent (80%) of its original specification.

## Section 3.2 — Accidental Damage from Handling (ADH)
Coverage includes accidental damage from handling resulting from an unexpected and unintentional external event, such as, drops and damage caused by liquid contact, subject to payment of the applicable service fee. For iPhone — Screen-Only Damage: US$29; All Other Damage: US$99. There is no service fee for hardware defects. Services for ADH expire once Apple has provided to you two (2) Service Events.

## Section 4.1 — Exclusions
This Plan does not cover: (a) normal wear and tear; (b) damage caused by reckless, abusive, willful or intentional conduct; cosmetic damage that does not affect functionality. Theft and misplacement are not covered under this Plan. (Under AppleCare+ with Theft and Loss only, Apple will arrange to replace Covered Equipment that is lost or stolen.)

## Section 5 — Making a Claim
To obtain service you must provide the Plan Agreement Number or Covered Device serial number, and proof of coverage.
