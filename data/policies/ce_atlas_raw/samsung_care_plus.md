# Samsung Care+ — Key Terms

> https://www.samsung.com/us/support/samsung-care-plus/service-contract-terms-and-conditions/
> (Short excerpts for analysis; not the full contract. Fee/limit specifics are
> set on the per-device Coverage Confirmation.)

## Coverage
If You submit a valid claim on a Product, notifying Us of a mechanical or electrical failure or other defect, We will arrange to service the Product. If You submit a valid claim notifying Us of a failure due to accidental damage from handling, We will arrange to service the Product. Spilled liquids are covered under the accidental-damage coverage type.

## Coverage Type — Theft & Loss
A Product that was lost, stolen or damaged (when Damage is not part of Your Coverage Type) is covered only under the Theft and Loss coverage type.

## Fees and Limits
Cracked screen repair is $29 per claim; the accidental-damage deductible is $99 to $199 depending on the device. Maximum Coverage Per Claim is the original purchase price of Your Product (excluding taxes). A maximum of three (3) accidental damage from handling claims applies in a 12-month period. Your coverage start date, Coverage Type, term, end date, price, and Service Fees are listed on Your Coverage Confirmation.

## Exclusions
This Service Contract excludes: Cosmetic damage, including but not limited to scratches, dents, or housing cracks; normal wear and tear; Any intentional dishonest, fraudulent or criminal act; a leaking Product battery (or any other leaking substance on or within the Product).

## Making a Claim
All claims for service must be reported as soon as reasonably possible via mycareplusclaim.com.
